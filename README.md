#### About me 

---

My name is Luca Hollman. I am a 3rd year Economics and Political Science student, currently studying at *Trinity College Dublin*. I am from Dublin, Ireland. I have a strong interest in social data science and coding, and I am currently learning R, Python, and Stata.
